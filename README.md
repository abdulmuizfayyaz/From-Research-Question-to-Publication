# From-Research-Question-to-Publication
I am truly honored to have been invited by the **IEEE Namal Student Branch** as a guest speaker for the webinar **“From Research Question to Publication.”**

It was a wonderful and highly engaging session, where I had the opportunity to share practical insights on transforming a research idea into a well-defined research question, conducting a systematic study, preparing a manuscript, selecting the right journal, handling peer review, and ultimately moving toward successful publication.

I am delighted that the session was successfully conducted and received such an enthusiastic response from the participants. My sincere appreciation to the **IEEE Namal Student Branch** for the kind invitation, warm hospitality, and excellent organization.

It was a pleasure interacting with aspiring researchers and students, and I hope the session contributed meaningfully to their research journey.
